    Calculation Methodology

    1. Head & energy price

    Total dynamic head is well depth (zero for surface water) plus operating pressure × 2.31 ft/psi. The state electricity or natural-gas price is converted to a cost per brake-kWh using the motor (90%) or gas-engine (27% × 302 kWh/Mcf) efficiency.

    2. Baseline & upgrades

    Applied water is the net crop water requirement divided by each system's application efficiency. Pumping energy = applied water × acres × head × 0.0853 kWh per ac-in-ft ÷ pump efficiency × cost per brake-kWh. Annual net savings = energy savings − the change in O&M versus the current system (+ crop revenue gain when irrigating a rainfed field).

    3. NPV, replacement & salvage

    Energy savings are discounted as a growing annuity at the energy escalation rate; O&M and revenue changes as level annuities. Investment is cost × acres × (1 − cost share). Systems that wear out before the horizon are replaced, and remaining life at the end is credited as salvage.

    4. Screens & ranking

    An upgrade must be more efficient than the current system, use no more water than the scenario allows, and have positive NPV. Remaining candidates are ranked by NPV. Priority is High when payback is within half the horizon.
