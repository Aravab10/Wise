Calculation Methodology

1. Applied Water

Applied water is calculated as net crop water requirement divided by irrigation-system efficiency. Baseline efficiency comes from the current system and each upgrade uses its SystemTech efficiency.

2. Total Dynamic Head & Pump Energy

Total dynamic head uses effective well depth plus operating pressure × 2.31. Groundwater uses full well depth, surface water uses zero well depth, and a mixed source scales well depth by the groundwater fraction.

3. Annual Net Savings

WISE compares baseline and upgrade pumping energy cost, then subtracts each upgrade's annual operation and maintenance cost across the full field.

4. NPV & Payback

NPV uses the model's growing-annuity treatment of annual net savings, discount rate, energy-price escalation, and analysis horizon, then subtracts net investment after incentives. Payback is net investment divided by annual net savings when recovery occurs within the selected horizon.
